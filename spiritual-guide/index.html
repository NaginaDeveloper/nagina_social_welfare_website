Allama Munir Ahmed Yusufi

Munir-e-Islam رحمۃ اللہ علیہ

A beloved scholar and Sufi guide whose teaching of sweet manner, patience, knowledge and charity continues to light the work of Nagina Social Welfare and Markaz Deen-e-Islam. May Allah elevate his rank. Ameen.

Blessed Spiritual Lineage

The blessed Yusufi lineages — Naqshbandi Mujaddidi in a longer and a shorter recitation, then Chishti Sabri Siraji Sardari, then Qadri Razawi — each read for the sake of the Prophet ﷺ, the Ahl al-Bayt, the Companions and the spiritual teachers (mashayikh), and ending in honour of the spiritual guide of the path (Pir-e-Tariqat), guide of sacred law (Rehbar-e-Shariat) Haji Muhammad Yusuf Ali Nagina رضی اللہ تعالیٰ عنہ.
