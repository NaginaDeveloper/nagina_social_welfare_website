User Manual

How to install the Android app, pin the parent web portal to your phone or computer, sign in, and use every page: messages, fees, leave, learning, and your own details.

1. Before you begin

The parent web portal is how parents follow Markaz Deen-e-Islam. You can use the Android app, or the parent web portal on a computer, tablet, or phone. You only see children the madrasa has linked to your account.

Sign in with the username the office gave you, not your email address. The password is the one you chose from the invite email, or the one you set after a reset. There is no public registration.

About these pictures. The portal pages were taken on 23 September 2026 while signed in as Test Father (username teststudent.father), linked to Test Student in TestClass. Attendance, messages, and learning are empty because nothing has been saved there yet. Fees show £50.00 due. The Chrome and Safari pictures are guides to the menus you will see. The icon those menus create is named Markaz Admin.

Parent web portal: https://admin.naginasocialwelfare.co.uk

2. Get the app

Android · available now

MDI Parent Portal

On an Android phone, install MDI Parent Portal from Google Play. It is published by Nagina Developer for Nagina Social Welfare UK. The office gives you the username. You cannot create an account from the store page.

Open the Play Store page
  1. On the phone, open the Play Store link above, or search for MDI Parent Portal.
  2. Check the crest and the name Nagina Developer, then tap Install.
  3. Open the app and sign in with the username and password the office gave you.

Support for the app is info@naginasocialwelfare.co.uk.

iPhone · coming soon

App Store version

A native iPhone app is on the way. It is not on the App Store yet, so there is nothing to download from Apple.

Until that app is released, open the parent web portal in Safari and add it to your Home Screen. The steps are in the next chapter. It then opens from an icon, the same way an app does, and you sign in with the same username.

3. Open it like an app

The parent web portal can also sit on your home screen or in the Dock. It opens in its own window, without the usual browser tabs. Use the same address and the same sign-in. The icon is labelled Markaz Admin.

Chrome on a computer

  1. Open Chrome and go to https://admin.naginasocialwelfare.co.uk.
  2. At the right of the address bar, click the install icon (a small screen with an arrow), then Install.
  3. If that icon is missing, click the three-dot menu, then Cast, save, and share, then Install page as app.
  4. The portal opens in its own window. You can also open it later from the Dock or the Start menu.

Chrome on Android

Use this when you want the parent web portal itself on the home screen, as well as, or instead of, the Play Store app.

  1. Open the parent web portal in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app. If you only see Add to Home screen, tap that instead.
  4. Confirm. The Markaz Admin icon appears on the home screen.

Safari on iPhone

This is the way to use the portal on an iPhone while the App Store app is still coming.

  1. Open the parent web portal in Safari. Other browsers on iPhone do not offer Add to Home Screen in the same way.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. The name shown is Markaz Admin. Tap Add.
  5. Open the new icon from the Home Screen and sign in.

Safari on a Mac

  1. Open the parent web portal in Safari.
  2. In the menu bar, choose File, then Add to Dock.
  3. Click Add.
  4. Open Markaz Admin from the Dock. It uses a simple window, without Safari’s full toolbar.

4. Open the web portal

The first screen asks you to choose a role. Parents use the gold Parent card. The other cards are for staff, students, collectors, quizzes, and the Halal barcode helper.

  1. Open https://admin.naginasocialwelfare.co.uk
  2. On the Parent card, press Continue.
  3. You land on the Parent Portal sign-in page. You can also go straight there at /login/parent.

5. Sign in

The sign-in card asks for username and password. Remember me keeps you signed in on this browser. Leave it unticked on a shared computer.

  1. Type your username.
  2. Type your password. The eye icon shows or hides it.
  3. Press Sign in.
  4. Home opens, with your name in the welcome banner.

A wrong username or password shows “Invalid username or password.” Check the username with the office before trying again and again.

After you are in, the left menu is Home, Messages, Fees, My child, Contact, and My Profile. Logout is at the top right and ends the session on this browser.

Forgot password

On the sign-in page, press Forgot password?. Enter the email or username on your parent record, then Send link. Open the email and follow the link to choose a new password. Use Cancel if you opened the box by mistake.

First-time invite

When the office creates your account, you receive an email with a setup link. Open that full link. It opens a page titled “Set up your parent account,” where you choose a password of at least 6 characters. After that you go to Home.

If the link is incomplete, the page says Invite link incomplete. Use the whole link from the email, or ask the office to send a new invitation. Do not try to type the setup address yourself without the token.

6. Home

Home is the summary after you sign in. It does not replace the detail pages. It points you to them.

  • Welcome banner. Your name, that you are a parent at the madrasa, and how many children are linked.
  • At a glance. One card per linked child: class, whether fees are up to date, this month’s attendance, and whether a madrasa message is waiting. View details opens My child.
  • Madrasa card. Address, Email, phone, WhatsApp, Give Sadaqah, and a link to the map page.
  • Quick access. Messages, Fees, My child, Request leave, Islamic Quiz, and Halal barcode.

Islamic Quiz and Halal barcode open other tools on the same parent web portal. The quiz is a timed practice quiz. The barcode helper looks up a shop label. It is a helper, not a Halal certificate.

7. Messages

Messages is the copy of emails the madrasa sends you. It is not a chat. You read notices here. You do not reply inside the portal.

This account has no messages yet, so the page shows an envelope and “Emails from the madrasa will appear here.”

When the office or a teacher sends mail about your child, the same note appears in this list. Typical notices are announcements, absence marks, fee receipts, fee reminders, fee waivers, term reports, and leave decisions.

Once messages exist, you can:

  1. Switch between All and Unread.
  2. If you have more than one child, filter by child, or show all children.
  3. Open a message to read it. Opening it marks it read.
  4. Use Mark as unread if you want it highlighted again, or Mark all read when the unread count is above zero.
  5. On a wide screen the list stays on the left and the open message on the right. On a phone, the message opens on its own.

An unread count also appears on Home and on the Messages item in the menu. Older emails sent before this inbox existed are not copied in automatically.

8. Fees

Fees shows money for the selected child. The three boxes are Total Fee, Total Paid, and Due. Under them is the payment history.

Test Student’s fee is £50.00, nothing has been paid, and £50.00 is due. Payment history says no payments have been recorded yet.

  1. Open Fees in the left menu, or the Fees tile on Home.
  2. If more than one child is linked, use Select child.
  3. Read the due amount. Green £0.00 means nothing is outstanding. An orange due amount means there is a balance to pay.
  4. When an amount is due, a Pay fees card appears with the balance filled in. Change the amount if you want to pay only part of it. Pay with card opens a secure SumUp page. The portal stays open in its own tab.
  5. After a successful card payment you return to Fees, with a short thank-you note.

Cash taken at the madrasa is recorded by staff. It shows in payment history after they save it. You cannot edit fee amounts yourself. The picture below was not used to start a payment.

9. My child

My child is the record book for one student. The child’s name sits above six tabs: Overview, Attendance, Leave, Madrasa life, Learning, and Term report. With more than one linked child, a switcher at the top changes who you are looking at. Everyone else’s children stay hidden.

These pages are for reading, except Leave, where you can ask for time away.

Overview

Four cards summarise the child: fees, attendance this month, the latest behaviour note, and the latest madrasa message. Each card is a shortcut into the fuller page.

Attendance

The month bar moves with the arrows. The totals are Present, Late, Absent, Informed, Uniform, Behaviour, and the attendance rate. The calendar marks the days the teacher has saved. Informed days are absences the madrasa already knows about, including approved leave.

September 2026 for Test Student has no marks, so every total is zero and the calendar days are blank.

Leave

Use this when your child will miss madrasa and you want the class teacher to approve it. Approved leave is stored as Informed attendance, so you are not sent an absence email for those days.

  1. Open My child, then Leave. You can also use the Request leave tile on Home.
  2. Press Request leave.
  3. Check the child. If several children are linked, tick each one who will be away. One submit covers the group.
  4. Set From and To. The form shows how many calendar days that is.
  5. Write a short reason, up to 500 characters.
  6. Press Submit request. Cancel closes the form without sending it.

The list then shows each request as pending, approved, rejected, or cancelled. You can cancel a request only while it is still pending. The teacher’s decision also arrives by email and in Messages.

Madrasa life

Madrasa life is a date-ordered list of what teachers have written about the child outside the formal term report: behaviour notes, activities, and participation. Newest entries are at the top. You cannot add or edit them.

Test Student has none yet, so the page says “No madrasa life records yet.”

Learning

Learning shows the teacher’s daily marks. It is view only. Tasks that were never attempted are hidden, so you see work that is completed or in progress.

The categories are Qaida, Quran, Surah Memorization, Duas, Islamic Education, and Participation. Open a category to see its list. Test Student’s Qaida list is empty.

Term report

Term report gathers the child’s name and class, the attendance rate, behaviour ratings for the term, and curriculum progress. It is the page to read when the teacher has saved a term summary. You can also receive that summary by email, which then appears in Messages.

11. My Profile

My Profile is the only place you edit your own record. The office still owns the username and the relationship (for example Father).

  1. Change full name, email, phones, or address if they are wrong. Separate extra phone numbers with commas.
  2. To change the email or the password, type your current password as well. A new password is optional. If you set one, confirm it in the last box.
  3. Press Save profile. The button stays quiet until something has changed.

Browser alerts asks this browser for permission to show a notification when something important happens. Email and Messages still arrive if you leave alerts off. If the browser has blocked notifications, allow them in the site settings and try the switch again.

Dark mode applies to the whole Parent Portal on this device only.

Export my data downloads a copy of the personal information the portal holds for you. Privacy notice (draft) opens the privacy text.

12. On a phone

On a narrow screen the left menu is replaced by a bar along the bottom: Home, Messages, Fees, My child, and More. The same pages are there. Cards and buttons stack so you do not have to slide sideways.

More opens a sheet with Contact and My Profile. Logout stays at the top right. The menu icon at the top left opens the full list as a drawer if you prefer that.
