Belief regarding the Awliya Allah

According to the consensus of Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Jama’ah, the Awliya (Aulia Karam) are the righteous friends of Allah — honoured through faith, taqwa, and sincere obedience. Loving and respecting them is part of authentic Sunni spirituality, always within the bounds of the Quran and Sunnah.

Allah Almighty declares in the Holy Quran that His Awliya shall know no fear nor grief. Through authentic Prophetic traditions, believers are taught to honour the pious, seek nearness to Allah through worship, and avoid enmity toward those whom Allah has befriended.
