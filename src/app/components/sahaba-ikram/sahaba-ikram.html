Belief regarding the Sahabah

According to the consensus of Sunni Islam (Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Jama’ah), all Companions (Sahabah) of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ are considered upright (‘adil), sincere, and the noble generation of the Muslim Ummah. Loving and respecting them is regarded as an integral part of Islamic faith.

In summary, while the Sahabah are not considered infallible (ma‘sum), they are revered as God-fearing, trustworthy role models for the entire Muslim nation — honoured alongside the Ahl al-Bayt in the creed of Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Jama’ah.
