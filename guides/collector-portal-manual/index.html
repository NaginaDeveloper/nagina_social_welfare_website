1. Before you begin

The Collector Portal is for the volunteers who empty Nagina Social Welfare UK’s charity collection boxes. Each box sits at a shop or venue and has a number and a QR code. For every box you collect, the portal records who took the cash, then the amount once it is counted, and sends the venue’s contact a text each time.

The charity office creates your account and gives you a username. The password is the one you chose from the invite email, or the one you set after a reset. There is no public registration.

This manual online: /guides/collector-portal-manual/

2. The Android app

Android · available now

NSW Collector Portal

On an Android phone, install NSW Collector Portal from Google Play. It is published by Nagina Developer for Nagina Social Welfare UK and has the same pages as the web portal. You cannot create an account from the store page.

Open the Play Store page
  1. On the phone, open the Play Store link above, or search for NSW Collector Portal.
  2. Check the crest and the publisher name, then tap Install.
  3. Open the app and sign in.
iPhone and computer · today

Use the web portal

Open admin.naginasocialwelfare.co.uk/login/collector in any browser and sign in. On an iPhone, add it to your Home Screen (chapter 3) so it opens from an icon like an app.

Sign in

  1. Open NSW Collector Portal from the home screen.
  2. Type your username and password. The eye icon shows or hides the password.
  3. Remember me is ticked already. Leave it ticked on your own phone, and the app opens straight to Home next time. Untick it on a shared phone.
  4. Tap Sign in. Forgot password? sends the same reset link as the web portal.

Home, Boxes, History, and Profile

A bar along the bottom holds Home, Boxes, History, and Profile. The round gold button in the middle opens the QR scanner. Sign Out is at the top right. The first time you scan, the phone asks to use the camera. Choose While using the app.

3. Open the web portal like an app

On any phone or computer, the collector web portal can sit on your home screen or in the Dock. It opens in its own window, without the usual browser tabs. Use the same address and the same sign-in. The icon is labelled Markaz Admin. This is the way to use the portal on an iPhone.

Chrome on a computer

  1. Open Chrome and go to https://admin.naginasocialwelfare.co.uk/login/collector.
  2. At the right of the address bar, click the install icon (a small screen with an arrow), then Install.
  3. If that icon is missing, click the three-dot menu, then Cast, save, and share, then Install page as app.
  4. The portal opens in its own window. You can also open it later from the Dock or the Start menu.

Chrome on Android

  1. Open the collector web portal in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app. If you only see Add to Home screen, tap that instead.
  4. Confirm. The Markaz Admin icon appears on the home screen.

Safari on iPhone

  1. Open the collector web portal in Safari. Other browsers on iPhone do not offer Add to Home Screen in the same way.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. The name shown is Markaz Admin. Tap Add.
  5. Open the new icon from the Home Screen and sign in.

Safari on a Mac

  1. Open the collector web portal in Safari.
  2. In the menu bar, choose File, then Add to Dock.
  3. Click Add.
  4. Open Markaz Admin from the Dock. It uses a simple window, without Safari’s full toolbar.

4. Open the web portal

The first screen asks you to choose a portal. Collectors use the orange Collector card. The other cards are for admins, teachers, parents, students, Islamic quizzes, and the Halal barcode helper.

  1. Open https://admin.naginasocialwelfare.co.uk
  2. On the Collector card, press Continue.
  3. You land on the collector sign-in page. You can also go straight there at https://admin.naginasocialwelfare.co.uk/login/collector.

5. Sign in

The Collector login card asks for your username and password. Remember me is ticked already and keeps you signed in on this browser. Untick it on a shared computer.

  1. Type your username.
  2. Type your password. The eye icon shows or hides it.
  3. Press Sign in.
  4. Home opens, with your name in the welcome card.

A wrong username or password shows “Invalid username or password.” If the office has switched your account off, sign-in says the account is inactive.

After you are in, the menu down the left side is Home, All Boxes, My Collections, and My Profile. Scan is at the top right of every page, and Sign Out at the bottom of the menu.

Forgot password

On the sign-in page, press Forgot password?. Enter the email or username on your record, then Send link. Open the email and follow the link to choose a new password. Your current password keeps working until you set the new one. Use Cancel if you opened the box by mistake.

First-time invite

When the office creates your account, an email with a setup link goes to the address on your record. Open that full link to choose a password. After that you go to Home. The same password works in the Android app.

If the link is incomplete, the page says Invite unavailable. Use the whole link from the email, or ask the office to send a new invitation.

6. Home

Home is your round at a glance.

  • Welcome card. Your name and username, how much you have collected this month and from how many collections, and how many boxes are still to collect.
  • Find a box. Type a box number and press Open box, or press Scan box QR (chapter 7).
  • Boxes to collect. The boxes not collected yet. Tap one to open it. See all boxes opens All Boxes.
  • This month. Your latest collections with venue, date and amount. My Collections opens the full month.
  • Browser alerts. Asks this browser for permission to show a notification. On iPhone, add the portal to your Home Screen first.

7. Find a box

Every box has a number on its label, such as 101. On Home, type the number under Find a box and press Open box. A number that matches no box shows “No box matches that code or number.”

Scan the box QR

Press Scan at the top right, Scan box QR on Home, or the gold button in the app. Hold the box’s QR code inside the frame. The box opens as soon as the code is read. The lightning button turns the torch on in a dark place. The first time, allow the camera when your phone or browser asks.

8. Collect from a box

A box page shows the box number, the venue, the contact person, and the box’s state: Not collected, Collected by you, or Receipt sent. The Ledger beside it records the box, venue, collector, and, once sent, the receipt amount and date. Collecting is two steps: take the cash, then send the receipt after counting.

Step 1: Collect Cash

  1. Open the box. It shows Not collected.
  2. When you take the cash out of the box, press Collect Cash.
  3. Check the box and venue in the Collect cash message, then press Collect. Cancel goes back without saving.

The box becomes Collected by you, and the venue’s contact gets a text saying who collected it and that the amount and receipt will follow after counting.

Step 2: Send Receipt

  1. Count the cash, then open the box again. It shows Collected by you.
  2. Type the amount under After counting, enter the amount, or tap £10, £20, £50 or £100.
  3. Press Send Receipt and confirm.

The amount is saved to the charity’s records and to My Collections, and the contact gets a text with the amount and a thank-you. Amounts over £10,000 must be recorded by the office.

If a text cannot be sent, the page says “Saved, but the text failed”. The collection is still saved. Press Try the text again.

Receipt sent

When the receipt is sent, the box shows Receipt sent with the amount and date, and says the box is done for this month. At the start of the next month it becomes Not collected again, ready for the next round.

9. All Boxes

All Boxes lists every charity box. Tabs filter them by state, with a count on each: Not collected, Collected, Receipt sent, and All. Search boxes finds a box by number, venue, or contact name. Tap a box to open it.

10. My Collections

My Collections is your own record for a month: the total collected, the number of entries, and each receipt grouped by day, with the box, venue, time and amount. The arrows beside the month move to the previous or next month.

11. My Profile

My Profile shows your name, username, email, phone, address, and your branch with its address and contact details. To change any of these, ask the charity office.

  • Password. Type your current password and the new one twice (at least 6 characters), then press Change password.
  • Browser alerts.Alert me on this device asks this browser for permission to show a notification.

Quran Academy, Manchester

A collector of the Manchester branch sees Quran Academy · Manchester as the branch, with Partington Community Centre, Manchester M31 4FL, +44 7872 340123 and qam@naginasocialwelfare.co.uk. Everything else in the portal works the same way.

12. Web portal on a phone

On a narrow screen the left menu becomes a bar along the bottom, the same as the app: Home, Boxes, the gold scan button, History, and Profile. Cards stack so you do not have to slide sideways. Sign Out is at the top right.
