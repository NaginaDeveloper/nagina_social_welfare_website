One portal serves both of our madrasas, in Peterborough and in Manchester.

1. Before you begin

The Student Portal is your own space at the madrasa. It is where you tick your daily checklist, learn with Let’s Learn Islam, collect stars, stickers and badges, read messages from your teachers, and look at your records. Your parents see the same records in the Parent Portal, and they are the ones who pay fees.

The madrasa office switches the portal on for you and gives you a username. Sign in with that username (or the email on your record), not with a name you make up. The password is the one you chose from the invite email, or the one you set after a reset. There is no public registration.

Our two branches

Nagina Social Welfare UK runs two madrasas. The portal knows which one you attend, so after you sign in, Home, Contact and every message show your own branch’s name, address and phone number. There is nothing to choose at sign-in.

2. The Android app

Android · available now

NSW Student Portal

On an Android phone or tablet, install NSW Student Portal from Google Play. It is published by Nagina Developer for Nagina Social Welfare UK and has the same pages as the web portal. Sign in with the username and password from the madrasa office. You cannot create an account from the store page.

Open the Play Store page
  1. On the phone, open the Play Store link above, or search for NSW Student Portal.
  2. Check the crest and the publisher name, then tap Install.
  3. Open the app and sign in.
iPhone and computer · today

Use the web portal

Open admin.naginasocialwelfare.co.uk/login/student in any browser and sign in. On a phone, add it to your Home Screen (chapter 3) so it opens from an icon like an app. Sign in once with Remember me ticked and it stays signed in on that device.

Sign in

  1. Open NSW Student Portal from the home screen.
  2. Type your username (or email) and password. The eye icon shows or hides the password.
  3. Remember me is ticked already. Leave it ticked on your own phone, and the app opens straight to Home next time. Untick it on a shared phone.
  4. Tap Sign in. Forgot password? sends the same reset link as the web portal.

Home, Checklist, and Learn

A bar along the bottom holds Home, Checklist, Learn, Messages, and More. Home shows the date, your name, your branch and class, today’s checklist ring, three quick jobs for today, the Today’s checklist and Let’s Learn Islam cards, and the Other things tiles. Checklist and Learn are the same pages as chapters 7 and 8.

Messages, More, and My Profile

Messages lists the notes your madrasa has sent you. More opens a sheet with My Records, Contact, and My Profile. My Profile is where you choose your colour, light or dark mode, sounds and motion, and change your password. Logout is at the top right of every page.

3. Open the web portal like an app

On any phone or computer, the student web portal can sit on your home screen or in the Dock. It opens in its own window, without the usual browser tabs. Use the same address and the same sign-in. The icon is labelled Markaz Admin.

Chrome on a computer

  1. Open Chrome and go to https://admin.naginasocialwelfare.co.uk.
  2. At the right of the address bar, click the install icon (a small screen with an arrow), then Install.
  3. If that icon is missing, click the three-dot menu, then Cast, save, and share, then Install page as app.
  4. The portal opens in its own window. You can also open it later from the Dock or the Start menu.

Chrome on Android

  1. Open the student web portal in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app. If you only see Add to Home screen, tap that instead.
  4. Confirm. The Markaz Admin icon appears on the home screen.

Safari on iPhone

  1. Open the student web portal in Safari. Other browsers on iPhone do not offer Add to Home Screen in the same way.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. The name shown is Markaz Admin. Tap Add.
  5. Open the new icon from the Home Screen and sign in.

Safari on a Mac

  1. Open the student web portal in Safari.
  2. In the menu bar, choose File, then Add to Dock.
  3. Click Add.
  4. Open Markaz Admin from the Dock. It uses a simple window, without Safari’s full toolbar.

4. Open the web portal

The first screen asks you to choose a portal. Students use the Student card. The other cards are for admins, teachers, parents, collectors, Islamic quizzes, and the Halal barcode helper.

  1. Open https://admin.naginasocialwelfare.co.uk
  2. On the Student card, press Continue.
  3. You land on the Student Portal sign-in page. You can also go straight there at https://admin.naginasocialwelfare.co.uk/login/student.

5. Sign in

The sign-in card asks for your username or email and your password. Remember me is ticked already and keeps you signed in on this browser. Untick it on a shared computer.

  1. Type your username or email.
  2. Type your password. The eye icon shows or hides it.
  3. Press Sign in.
  4. Home opens, with your name in the welcome card and your branch and class beneath it.

A wrong username or password shows “Invalid username or password.” Check the username with the office before trying again and again. If the office has switched your portal off, sign-in does not work until it is switched on again.

After you are in, the left menu is Home, Daily checklist, Let’s Learn Islam, Messages, My Records, Contact, and My Profile. Logout is at the top right and ends the session on this browser.

Forgot password

On the sign-in page, press Forgot password?. Enter the email or username on your record, then Send link. Open the email and follow the link to choose a new password. Use Cancel if you opened the box by mistake. If the email on your record is a parent’s address, ask them to open it.

First-time invite

When the office switches your portal on, an email with a setup link goes to the address on your record. Open that full link. It opens a page titled “Set up your student account,” where you choose a password. After that you go to Home. The same password will work in the Android app.

If the link is incomplete, the page says Invite link incomplete. Use the whole link from the email, or ask the office to send a new invitation.

6. Home

Home is “your day at a glance.” It shows what to do today and where you have got to.

  • Welcome card. Today’s date, a greeting, your name, your branch and class, and a ring that counts how many checklist items you have done today. My rewards opens your stars and stickers.
  • Today. Three quick jobs: Tick your Morning list, Learn today, and Read a dua. Each one opens the right page.
  • Today’s checklist card. Your score out of 20, the next item to tick, your streak, and Start today’s checklist.
  • Let’s Learn Islam card. Your learning streak, level and XP, with Continue to carry on from where you stopped.
  • Messages banner. Appears when you have unread messages. Tap it to read them.
  • Alerts card. Offers browser alerts for messages and announcements. Enable alerts turns them on. Not now hides the card.
  • Other things. Tiles for My Rewards, My Records, Request Leave, Islamic Quiz, and Halal Barcode.
  • Your card and My madrasa. Your attendance this month and latest message, then your branch’s address, Email, phone, WhatsApp, Give Sadaqah, and directions.

Islamic Quiz is a timed practice quiz. The Halal Barcode helper looks up a shop label. It is a helper, not a Halal certificate.

7. Daily checklist

The daily checklist is your list of good habits for each day, in three parts: Morning, Day, and Evening. It covers duas, the five prayers, being good at school and madrasa, helping at home, learning something new, and a good deed. Twenty items in all.

  1. Open Daily checklist in the menu, or tap Start today’s checklist on Home.
  2. Pick a day in the date strip. Today is selected first. Days you finished carry a star badge, and the number under a day shows how many items you ticked.
  3. Tap the circle next to an item when you have done it. Items with Read dua show the dua to read.
  4. Finish a whole part (all of Morning, all of Day, or all of Evening) to earn a star. Three stars in one day is a golden day.
  5. Keep going day after day to build a streak. The card at the top shows today’s score, stars, and streak.
  6. Daily Supplications at the top lists all the duas to read.

You can tick today and yesterday. Older days are for reading. Your parents see the same checklist in the Parent Portal and can tick it with you. Only a parent can set the behaviour for the day.

8. Let’s Learn Islam

Let’s Learn Islam is a set of short lessons in English and Urdu. The modules sit on a path. You start with the first one, and the next unlocks when you pass. The header shows your streak (days learned in a row), your level, and your XP for the week, with a weekly goal.

  1. Open Let’s Learn Islam in the menu, or Continue on Home.
  2. Choose English or اردو at the top. You can switch at any time.
  3. Press Start on the first module (Our beliefs), or tap a module on the path. Locked modules open after you pass the one before.
  4. Work through the lesson cards, then take the module check. Pass it to unlock the next module and earn your certificate.

The settings icon next to the weekly bar sets the sounds and the daily reminder. Review brings back cards you have learned so you do not forget them.

Lessons and cards

A lesson is a short run of cards. The bar at the top fills as you go. A Q&A card asks a question: think, then press Show answer and say whether you knew it. Other cards show a fact, a dua, or a picture. Press the speaker icon to hear the card read aloud, and the cross at the top left to leave the lesson. Your place is saved.

Badges and certificates

From the path, My progress shows four tabs: Badges (thirteen learning badges such as First steps, 3-day streak, and Module master), Class (how your class is doing), Stats, and Certificates. A certificate appears here for every module you pass. Your parents can download it from the Parent Portal too.

9. My Rewards

My Rewards collects everything you have earned. Open it from the My rewards chip on Home, the Other things tile, or My Profile.

  • Star streak, Stars this week, and Learn level at the top.
  • Star Days. A strip of this week with the stars you earned each day, and how many more stars reach this week’s goal.
  • Stickers. Twelve checklist stickers, for example First star, Golden day, 3 star days, Five prayers, and Morning champion. Each one tells you how to earn it.
  • Learn badges. The thirteen Let’s Learn Islam badges, with Class & stats.
  • Certificates and Star of the Week, when your teacher awards it.

10. Messages

Messages are notes from your madrasa: a welcome, a well-done from your teacher, a leave decision, or a certificate. It is not a chat. You read notes here. You do not reply inside the portal.

  1. Switch between All and Unread.
  2. Open a note to read it. Opening it marks it read. On a wide screen the list stays on the left and the open note on the right.
  3. Use Mark as unread to highlight it again, or Mark all read when the unread count is above zero.

An unread count also appears on Home and on the Messages item in the menu.

11. My Records

My Records is your record book, the same one your parents see. It has six tabs: Overview, Attendance, Leave, Madrasa life, Learning, and Term report. These pages are for reading, except Leave, where you can ask for time away.

Overview

Four cards: fees outstanding, attendance this month, the latest daily log from your teacher, and the latest madrasa message. The fees card is for information only. Fees are paid by your parents in the Parent Portal.

Attendance

The month bar moves with the arrows. The totals are Present, Late, Absent, Informed, Uniform, Behaviour, and the attendance rate. The calendar marks the days the teacher has saved, and the list under it shows each saved day. Informed days are absences the madrasa already knows about, including approved leave.

Leave

Use this when you will miss madrasa and want your class teacher to know. Approved leave is stored as Informed attendance.

  1. Open My Records, then Leave. You can also use the Request Leave tile on Home.
  2. Press Request leave.
  3. Set From and To. The form shows how many calendar days that is.
  4. Write a short reason, up to 500 characters.
  5. Press Submit request. Cancel closes the form without sending it.

The list then shows each request as pending, approved, rejected, or cancelled. Your teacher’s decision arrives in Messages. Your parents can see the request too.

Madrasa life

Madrasa life is a date-ordered list of what your teachers have written about you outside the term report: behaviour notes, activities, and participation. Newest entries are at the top.

Learning

Learning shows your teacher’s daily marks. It is view only. The categories are Qaida, Quran, Surah Memorization, Duas, Islamic Education, and Participation. Open a category to see its list. Tasks that were never attempted are hidden.

Term report

Term report gathers your name and class, the latest term, your attendance rate, curriculum progress, and the term ratings for Effort in Class, Behaviour, Presentation, and Punctuality.

12. Contact

Contact is your branch’s public details. Open in Google Maps starts directions. Email, the phone number, and WhatsApp reach the office. NaginaSocialWelfare.co.uk opens the public website and Give Sadaqah opens the donations page.

Quran Academy, Manchester

A student at Quran Academy sees the Manchester details instead: Partington Community Centre, Manchester M31 4FL, phone and WhatsApp +44 7872 340123, and email qam@naginasocialwelfare.co.uk. Everything else in the portal works the same way.

13. My Profile

My Profile is “My Space.” It shows your star days this month, your star streak, and your learn level, with a shortcut to My Rewards. Then come the settings that make the portal yours.

  • My colour. Pick one of nine colours. It colours Home, buttons and the menu everywhere you sign in.
  • Appearance. Light or Dark mode, for this device.
  • Sounds and motion. Tick sounds and celebrations follow you to every device. Vibration and Reduce motion are set on this device.
  • My details. Your name, class, username, email, and date of birth. These are read only. Ask the madrasa office to correct any of them.
  • Change password. Type your current password and the new one twice, then press Change password. The Privacy notice is linked underneath.
  • Browser alerts. Asks this browser for permission to show a notification when something important happens. On iPhone, add the portal to your Home Screen first.
  • Madrasa card. Your branch’s address, Email, phone, WhatsApp, Give Sadaqah, and directions.

14. Web portal on a phone

On a narrow screen the left menu is replaced by a bar along the bottom: Home, Checklist, Learn, Messages, and More. The same pages are there. Cards and tiles stack so you do not have to slide sideways.

More opens a sheet with My Records, Contact, and My Profile. Logout stays at the top right. The menu icon at the top left opens the full list as a drawer if you prefer that.
